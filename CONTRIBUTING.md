# Contributing corrections

This repository welcomes evidence-based corrections and source suggestions.

When opening an issue, include:

1. the publication title and version;
2. the section or page concerned;
3. the statement that may need correction;
4. a stable link or complete bibliographic reference supporting the correction;
5. a concise proposed revision.

Devotional tradition, historical documentation, astronomical calculation, and modern empirical evidence are treated as distinct evidence categories. Please identify which category supports the proposed change.

Material corrections are reviewed editorially and, when accepted, are incorporated into a new version of the canonical paper and Zenodo record. Minor typographic or repository-only fixes do not create a new DOI version.
